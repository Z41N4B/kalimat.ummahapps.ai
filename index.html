Tap a word to hear it

Tap Learning on the words you are working on. The game only uses words marked Learning. Tap Plural on a card to see and hear its plural. When you know a word really well, tap Known well: it leaves the game and moves to the Known well tab. A word also moves there by itself when you get it right first time 5 times in a row in the games or in story questions. Tap My Learning words to see only the words you are practising. Use the groups, search and filter to focus on what you are working on.

Sound and broken plurals: a short note

Sound plural. The word stays whole and gets an ending. Men, or a group with a man in it, take ـُونَ (or ـِينَ after فِي, مِنْ and as the object). Women, and many things, take ـَات: مُعَلِّم ← مُعَلِّمُونَ, مُعَلِّمَة ← مُعَلِّمَات, سَيَّارَة ← سَيَّارَات.

Broken plural. The inside of the word changes, like man and men in English: كِتَاب ← كُتُب, وَلَد ← أَوْلَاد, بَيْت ← بُيُوت. There are many patterns and no rule tells you which one a word takes, so learn the plural together with the word.

Good to know. The plural of a thing (not people) is treated like one “her” word: هَذِهِ كُتُبٌ (these are books), الْبُيُوتُ كَبِيرَةٌ (the houses are big).

      About Kalimat

      Kalimat (كَلِمَات, "words") is a free Arabic vocabulary trainer for beginners. It has over 400 everyday Modern Standard Arabic words in 16 groups: basics, people, places, home and school, food and drink, nature and animals, the body, colours, describing words, actions, numbers, time, greetings, clothes, toys and play, and feelings. The Stories tab uses these words in 15 short stories, so you meet them again in real sentences.

      Every word is written with full harakat (vowel marks) and can be heard aloud at normal or slow speed. Most nouns and describing words show their plural, marked as a sound plural or a broken plural. Words are sorted from beginner to advanced. Mark the words you are learning, practise them by picking the picture, picking the plural or saying them from memory, and move them to Known well when you have mastered them, then revise them now and then so they stay fresh. You can also add words from your own lessons.

      Is Kalimat free?

      Yes. Kalimat is free, needs no account and runs in your web browser.

      How does Kalimat pronounce the Arabic words?

      It uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. For the best pronunciation, add an Arabic voice in your phone or computer's speech or language settings, then choose it in the Voice menu.

      Does Kalimat teach Arabic plurals?

      Yes. Most nouns and describing words show their plural and say whether it is a sound plural or a broken plural, and there is a game for saying the plural from memory.

      Does Kalimat have Arabic stories?

      Yes. The Stories tab has 15 short, fully vowelled stories made mostly from words in Kalimat. You listen first with pictures, read along and tap any word for its meaning, answer questions in Arabic, then tell the story yourself.

      Where is my progress saved?

      Your Learning and Known well lists and your own words are saved in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. Up to three learners can share one device, each with their own progress.

      Who is Kalimat for?

      Beginners of any age learning Modern Standard Arabic, including children, home-schoolers and adults studying for Quranic or Madinah-style Arabic classes.
