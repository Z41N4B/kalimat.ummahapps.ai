      About Kalimat

      Kalimat (كَلِمَات, "words") is a free Arabic vocabulary trainer for beginners. It has over 400 everyday Modern Standard Arabic words in 16 groups: basics, people, places, home and school, food and drink, nature and animals, the body, colours, describing words, actions, numbers, time, greetings, clothes, toys and play, and feelings.

      Every word is written with full harakat (vowel marks) and can be heard aloud at normal or slow speed. Most nouns show their plural, marked as a sound plural or a broken plural. Mark words as learned, practise them in the reading quiz or by saying them from memory, and move them to Known well when you have mastered them. You can also add words from your own lessons.

      Is Kalimat free?

      Yes. Kalimat is free, needs no account and runs in your web browser.

      How does Kalimat pronounce the Arabic words?

      It uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. For the best pronunciation, add an Arabic voice in your phone or computer's speech or language settings, then choose it in the Voice menu.

      Does Kalimat teach Arabic plurals?

      Yes. Most nouns show their plural and say whether it is a sound plural or a broken plural, and there is a game for saying the plural from memory.

      Where is my progress saved?

      Learned words, known-well words and your own words are saved in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

      Who is Kalimat for?

      Beginners of any age learning Modern Standard Arabic, including children, home-schoolers and adults studying for Quranic or Madinah-style Arabic classes.
